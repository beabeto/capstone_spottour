4.5 Evaluation Method and Tools

To evaluate the quality and effectiveness of SpotTour: An Interactive Platform for Tourism and Community Engagement, the researchers used the ISO/IEC 25010 Software Product Quality Model. The evaluation was based on the SpotTour System Evaluation Survey administered through Google Forms. The following characteristics were used:

  • Functional Suitability – the ability of the system to provide the required features for tourism information, booking, online payment, reviews, ratings, favorites, bookmarks, checklists, map location, and day planning.
  • Usability – the ease with which travellers, owners, and administrators can understand, learn, and operate the system.
  • Reliability and Performance – the ability of the system to work consistently during browsing, booking, and account-related tasks, and to load and respond within an acceptable time on both desktop and mobile devices.
  • Security – the protection of user accounts, personal information, and payment-related pages through login, confirmation messages, and role-based access.
  • Usefulness and Tourism Impact – the ability of the system to improve access to tourism information, promote local establishments, and support community engagement in Surigao City.

Respondents

The respondents were selected through purposive sampling and included the intended users of SpotTour. A total of 25 respondents participated in the evaluation: two (2) tourism office / administrator personnel, four (4) business owners, eleven (11) travellers / tourists, six (6) local residents, and two (2) student / faculty evaluators.

Evaluation Tools

The following tools were used during the evaluation:

  1. Survey Questionnaire – a Google Form survey was prepared for all respondent groups. Section 1 gathered the respondent profile (age, gender, role, and frequency of using tourism-related websites or apps). Sections 2 to 6 measured Functional Suitability, Usability, Reliability and Performance, Security, and Usefulness and Tourism Impact using a five-point Likert scale. Section 7 gathered overall satisfaction and open-ended comments.
  2. System Testing / Walkthrough – respondents were asked to use the main features of SpotTour that were relevant to their role, such as browsing listings, booking, payment, account management, and content management.
  3. Observation – the researchers observed how respondents used the system in order to identify difficulties, errors, and areas for improvement.
  4. Comments and Suggestions – open-ended questions in the Google Form allowed respondents to state what they liked most, what should be improved, and other suggestions.

Evaluation Procedure

The researchers first oriented the respondents on the purpose of the study and the features of SpotTour. The respondents then used the system according to their role and answered the Google Form survey afterward. The survey used a five-point Likert scale with the following values: 1 = Strongly Disagree, 2 = Disagree, 3 = Neutral, 4 = Agree, and 5 = Strongly Agree.

Data Analysis

The survey results were analyzed using the weighted mean, computed as:

WM = Σ (f × w) / N

where WM is the weighted mean, f is the number of respondents who selected a rating, w is the value of the rating, and N is the total number of respondents. The weighted mean was computed per group of respondents and for all respondents combined, and was interpreted using the following scale:

Reliability and Performance obtained an overall mean of 4.24, interpreted as Strongly Agree. The respondents agreed that the system worked properly during browsing, booking, and account-related tasks, loaded within an acceptable time, and could be used on both desktop and mobile devices.

Security obtained an overall mean of 4.33, interpreted as Strongly Agree. The respondents believed that login and account features were appropriate, personal information was handled carefully, payment pages appeared trustworthy, and confirmation messages were provided for important actions.

The respondents agreed that SpotTour can improve access to tourism information in Surigao City, help promote local spots, hotels, and restaurants, and support community engagement through reviews and ratings.

Comments and Suggestions

The respondents stated that they liked the centralized presentation of tourist spots, hotels, and restaurants, the booking and payment process, and the interactive features such as reviews, ratings, favorites, and map location. Several respondents also mentioned that the system was easy to navigate and useful for planning a visit around Surigao City.

For improvement, some respondents suggested adding a native mobile application, more payment options, faster loading on mobile data, and more detailed owner dashboards for booking and sales reports. A few also recommended additional travel recommendations and stronger notification reminders for upcoming reservations.

Summary of Findings

Overall, the evaluation results indicate that SpotTour is functional, user-friendly, reliable, secure, and useful as a digital tourism and community-engagement platform. The grand mean of 4.33, interpreted as Strongly Agree, shows that the system met the expectations of travellers, residents, owners, tourism personnel, and academic evaluators. The findings also confirm that SpotTour can help improve tourism information access, promote local establishments, and support a more organized tourism experience in Surigao City.
